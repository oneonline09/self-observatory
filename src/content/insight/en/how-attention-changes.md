I've started to suspect that *attention* isn't a tool I use, but the **material** I'm made of. Whatever I attend to long enough slowly becomes my world.

> We don't become what we think. We become what we keep looking at.

The frightening part is that attention can be *drained* without our noticing — by notifications, by hurry, by the habit of scrolling. Each time, a small piece of the ability to stay-with-one-thing is worn away.

So lately I practice something simple: looking at one thing longer than it takes to see it. A canopy of leaves. A face. A question with no answer yet.
