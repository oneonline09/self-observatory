I used to think slowness was a luxury. Now I think it's a **discipline**.

Slowness doesn't mean doing less. It means giving one thing *enough time* to reveal its true shape — the way tea needs time to steep, the way a friendship needs many returns.

The hardest part of slowness is bearing the feeling that you're missing out. But perhaps *missing out* is exactly the price of being fully present in one place.
