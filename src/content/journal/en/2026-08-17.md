      And then there are people who think we're just talking nonsense.

      an opinion.


      And many of those opinions come with some degree of judgment, hostility,
      or even contempt.

      Meanwhile, we haven't actually done anything to hurt or offend anyone.


      That made me realize something: people are naturally very quick to judge
      and evaluate one another. That's just human nature. So why bother
      exhausting ourselves worrying about it?

      Perhaps the only thing we can do is be mindful of our actions, our words,
      and the way we conduct ourselves. Don't give people too many reasons to
      talk badly about you, and that's enough.


      So don't be foolish enough to try to please everyone.


      If something feels right and reasonable to us, then do it. Don't get
      pulled around by what other people say. Nine people will have ten
      different opinions; at some point you won't even know whether you're
      dealing with saints or devils.


      As long as we ourselves are clear, that's enough.


      And if we're not clear yet, then we can slowly find our way through
      it—try, make mistakes, learn from them, and minimize the consequences.
