This morning I sat at the café down the alley longer than usual. Not for the coffee, but for **the way people greet one another** — a nod, an offhand question about a child, about last night's rain.

> Public life doesn't live in the grand square. It lives in the small pockets where strangers briefly stop being strangers.

In Đà Nẵng, that *small pocket* is often the sidewalk. A plastic chair turned toward the street is an invitation. I wonder: as the city grows, where will those chairs go?

Maybe what I'm really recording here isn't the café, but a **hypothesis**: that care between people needs a physical place to happen. Take away that place, and we take away more than we think.
