A good community space is rarely loud. It simply *shows up* — doors open, lights on, someone who remembers your name.

Last night I noticed something small: the facilitator wasn't trying to make things fun. She was **making room** for people to find each other. There's a large difference between *organizing joy* and *clearing space for joy to happen*.

I carried this question home: is a community built from events, or from the habit of coming back?
